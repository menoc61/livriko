Features

Livriko provides two mobile applications — a User App for passengers and a Driver App for drivers. Both are built with React Native and share a common technology stack while serving distinct purposes.

User App Features

The User App is the passenger-facing application that provides a complete ride-hailing, rental, and delivery experience:

  • Ride Booking — Request rides with real-time fare estimation, driver matching, and GPS tracking from pickup to destination. Choose from multiple vehicle types including economy, comfort, and premium.
  • Schedule Rides — Book rides in advance for a specific date and time. Perfect for airport transfers and important appointments where punctuality matters.
  • Vehicle Rental — Rent vehicles by the hour or day with flexible packages and deposit management. Includes insurance options and mileage tracking.
  • Parcel Delivery — Send and track packages with real-time delivery status, proof of delivery, and recipient confirmation.
  • Multiple Payments — Pay with cash, credit card, digital wallets, or in-app wallet balance. Supports saved payment methods for quick checkout.
  • Ratings and Reviews — Rate your driver and provide feedback after each ride to maintain service quality across the platform.
  • Ride History — View past rides, receipts, and trip details at any time. Export receipts for expense reporting.
  • Wallet — Top up your wallet balance and use it for ride payments with automatic deductions. View transaction history and manage funds.
  • Coupons and Promotions — Apply discount codes and promotional offers at checkout for reduced fares.
  • SOS Emergency — Quick access to emergency services with one-tap SOS button for passenger safety.
  • In-App Support — Contact support directly from the app for any ride-related issues or inquiries. Includes chat and call options.
  • Multi-Language Support — Switch between multiple languages and regions for a localized experience.

Driver App Features

The Driver App provides drivers with all the tools they need to manage their rides and earnings:

  • Receive Ride Requests — Get real-time ride requests with pickup location, destination, and fare details. View request frequency and acceptance rate.
  • Accept / Reject Rides — Accept or reject incoming ride requests based on your availability, location, and destination preferences.
  • Turn-by-Turn Navigation — Integrated Google Maps navigation for efficient route planning and driving directions. Includes traffic awareness.
  • Earnings Dashboard — Track daily, weekly, and monthly earnings with detailed trip breakdowns. View charts and trends over time.
  • Driver Wallet — Manage your earnings balance, request withdrawals, and view transaction history. Supports multiple payout methods.
  • Online / Offline Toggle — Go online to receive ride requests or offline when taking a break. The app respects your availability status.
  • Ride History — Review completed trips, earnings per ride, and customer ratings received. Filter by date range.
  • Background Location — Continuous location sharing for ride matching and real-time tracking by passengers. Required for driver visibility on the map.
  • SOS Emergency — Quick access to emergency services with one-tap SOS button for driver safety. Includes location sharing with emergency contacts.
  • Document Management — Upload and manage driver documents such as license, insurance, and vehicle registration. Receive expiry reminders.
  • Fleet Management — For fleet owners, manage multiple vehicles and drivers from a single dashboard.
