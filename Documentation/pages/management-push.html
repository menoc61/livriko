Push Notifications

Send targeted push notifications to riders and drivers via Firebase Cloud Messaging — reach your users instantly with promotions, updates, and alerts.

Overview

Push notifications are one of the most effective communication channels for engaging riders and drivers. Livriko integrates with Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver reliable, real-time push notifications to both iOS and Android devices.

Use push notifications for:

  • Promotions — Announce discount codes, seasonal offers, and referral bonuses.
  • Operational Alerts — Notify drivers of high-demand zones or service disruptions.
  • Engagement — Re-engage inactive riders with personalized offers and reminders.
  • Safety — Send emergency alerts and critical safety information to all users.
Firebase Integration

Push notifications in Livriko are powered by Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Ensure your Firebase Messaging is properly configured before sending notifications. The platform uses FCM topics for targeted delivery.

Notification Types

Livriko supports both automated (system-triggered) and manual (admin-initiated) push notifications. Automated notifications are sent automatically at specific points in the ride lifecycle.

Notification Trigger Recipient Auto / Manual
Ride Request Rider requests a new ride Nearby drivers Automatic
Ride Accepted Driver accepts the ride request Rider Automatic
Driver Arriving Driver is within 2 minutes of pickup Rider Automatic
Ride Started Driver taps "Start Ride" Rider Automatic
Ride Completed Driver taps "End Ride" Rider & Driver Automatic
Payment Received Payment is processed successfully Driver Automatic
Promotional Admin sends a manual notification Targeted audience Manual

Sending a Push Notification

To manually send a push notification from the admin panel:

1

Navigate to Push Notifications

Go to Livriko Management → Push Notifications. You will see a list of previously sent notifications and their delivery status.

2

Click "Send New Notification"

Open the notification composer by clicking the Send New Notification button.

3

Select Target Audience

Choose who receives the notification (see Targeting Options below). This determines the FCM topic used for delivery.

4

Compose the Message

Enter the notification Title (headline) and Message (body text). Optionally add an image and a deep link URL that opens when the notification is tapped.

5

Send or Schedule

Click Send to deliver immediately, or choose Schedule to send at a specific date and time.

Targeting Options

Livriko uses FCM topics to segment notification delivery. Select the appropriate audience to ensure notifications reach the right users.

Target Description FCM Topic
All Users Every rider and driver on the platform receives the notification. all_users
All Riders Only rider accounts receive the notification. riders
All Drivers Only driver accounts receive the notification. drivers
Specific Zone Users in a selected zone receive the notification. Ideal for city-specific promotions. zone_{zone_id}
Vehicle Type Drivers of a specific vehicle type receive the notification. vehicle_{type_id}
Test Before Broadcasting

Always send a test notification to your own device before broadcasting to all users. This ensures the message content, formatting, and deep links work correctly. A misconfigured notification sent to thousands of users can damage trust and engagement.

Bulk Notifications

When sending notifications to large audiences, Livriko handles the delivery in batches to avoid overwhelming the FCM API and to ensure reliable delivery. The system automatically retries failed deliveries and logs the outcome for each device token.

  • Batch Processing — Large audiences are split into smaller batches and sent sequentially.
  • Retry Logic — Failed deliveries (due to network issues, device offline, etc.) are retried up to 3 times.
  • Delivery Tracking — The notification history shows the number of successful and failed deliveries for each broadcast.

Scheduling Notifications

Scheduled notifications allow you to plan campaigns in advance. This is particularly useful for:

  • Weekend Promotions — Schedule Friday evening notifications for Saturday morning offers.
  • Holiday Campaigns — Prepare notifications for holidays and special events ahead of time.
  • Peak Hour Alerts — Schedule driver incentives for rush hour periods automatically.

To schedule a notification, select the Schedule option in the composer and set the desired date and time. The notification will be queued and delivered automatically at the scheduled time.

Timezone Handling

Scheduled notifications are delivered in the timezone configured in Platform Settings. Ensure the timezone is set correctly to avoid notifications being sent at unexpected times for your users.

Best Practices

  1. Keep Messages Short — Push notifications have limited screen space. Keep titles under 50 characters and body text under 150 characters for optimal readability.
  2. Personalize When Possible — Use Notification Templates with variables like {{user_name}} to create personalized experiences.
  3. Don't Over-Notify — Sending too many notifications leads to app uninstalls. Limit promotional notifications to 2–3 per week.
  4. Use Rich Media — Include relevant images to increase engagement. Visual notifications stand out more than text-only ones.
  5. Leverage Deep Links — Add URLs that open specific screens in the app (e.g., a promotion page, wallet top-up, or ride history).
  6. Measure Results — Monitor delivery rates and user engagement. Low delivery rates may indicate stale device tokens that need cleanup.
Engagement Tip
