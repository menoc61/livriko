Fleet Manager

Organize drivers into fleets and assign dedicated fleet managers for scalable, structured operations across your platform.

Why Fleet Management Matters

As your platform grows, managing individual drivers becomes increasingly time-consuming. Fleet management introduces a hierarchical structure where a fleet manager oversees a group of drivers and vehicles, handling day-to-day operations while you focus on platform strategy.

Fleet management is ideal for:

  • Large Operations — Platforms with hundreds or thousands of drivers benefit from delegated management.
  • Corporate Partners — Taxi companies, transport businesses, and corporate fleets that operate multiple vehicles.
  • Regional Management — Assigning fleet managers to specific geographic zones for localized oversight.
  • Scalability — Adding new drivers and vehicles without increasing admin workload proportionally.

Fleet Manager Fields Reference

When creating or editing a fleet, the following fields define the fleet and its manager.

Field Type Description
Fleet Name Text A unique name for the fleet (e.g., "City Express", "Premium Fleet Alpha"). Used for identification across the admin panel and fleet dashboard.
Manager Name Text Full name of the fleet manager responsible for this fleet.
Email Email Fleet manager's email address for login credentials and notifications.
Phone Phone Contact phone number for the fleet manager.
Description Text A brief description of the fleet — its focus area, vehicle types, or operational scope.
Status Toggle Enable or disable the fleet. Disabled fleets cannot have drivers assigned and are hidden from the fleet dashboard.

Creating a Fleet Manager Account

1

Navigate to Fleet Manager

Log in to the admin panel and go to Livriko Management → Fleet Manager.

2

Click "Add New Fleet"

Click the Add New Fleet button to open the fleet creation form.

3

Enter Fleet Details

Fill in the fleet name, manager name, email, phone, and description. Ensure the email is valid — login credentials will be sent to this address.

4

Save the Fleet

Click Save. The fleet manager receives an email with their login credentials and can access the fleet dashboard immediately.

5

Assign Drivers

Go to the fleet's detail page and assign drivers from the list of available approved drivers. Each driver can belong to only one fleet at a time.

Driver Assignment

Drivers must be in Approved status before they can be assigned to a fleet. If a driver is still pending verification, complete the verification process first.

Fleet Manager Dashboard

Once a fleet is set up, the fleet manager has access to a dedicated dashboard that provides comprehensive visibility into their fleet's operations.

Dashboard Features

Rides Analytics

View total rides completed, average ride duration, peak hours, and ride completion rates for the fleet.

Revenue Reports

Track total revenue generated by the fleet, earnings per driver, commission deductions, and net income.

Driver Performance

Monitor individual driver ratings, acceptance rates, cancellation rates, and average response times.

Live Locations

See real-time positions of all fleet drivers on a map, filtered by status and vehicle type.

Driver-Fleet Association

Drivers assigned to a fleet operate under the fleet manager's oversight while retaining their individual driver accounts. The association provides several benefits:

  • Centralized Earnings — Fleet-level earnings are aggregated in the Fleet Wallet, simplifying accounting and payout processes.
  • Performance Tracking — Fleet managers can compare driver performance within their fleet and identify top performers and underperformers.
  • Operational Control — Fleet managers can communicate with their drivers, manage schedules, and handle day-to-day issues without admin intervention.
  • Document Management — Track vehicle registration, insurance, and license expiry dates for all fleet vehicles in one place.
One Fleet Per Driver

A driver can only belong to one fleet at a time. To move a driver to a different fleet, you must first remove them from their current fleet before assigning them to a new one.

Fleet-Level Analytics

The fleet dashboard provides detailed analytics that help fleet managers optimize their operations.

Metric Description Available To
Total Rides Number of rides completed by the fleet within a selected time period. Fleet Manager & Admin
Revenue Total fare amount collected from rides, before commission deduction. Fleet Manager & Admin
Net Earnings Revenue after platform commission and subscription fees are deducted. Fleet Manager & Admin
Average Rating Mean customer rating across all drivers in the fleet. Fleet Manager & Admin
Acceptance Rate Percentage of ride requests accepted vs. total requests received by the fleet. Fleet Manager & Admin
Cancellation Rate Percentage of rides cancelled by fleet drivers after acceptance. Fleet Manager & Admin
Online Hours Total hours fleet drivers spent online and available for rides. Fleet Manager & Admin
Active Vehicles Number of vehicles currently assigned to the fleet with valid documents. Admin only

Fleet Manager Permissions

Fleet managers have a limited set of permissions compared to platform admins. They can manage their fleet's operations but cannot access platform-wide settings or other fleets' data.

Send push notifications

Benefits of Fleet Management

  1. Reduced Admin Workload — Fleet managers handle driver onboarding, scheduling, and performance monitoring, freeing up platform administrators for strategic tasks.
  2. Better Accountability — Clear ownership of driver groups means issues are resolved faster and performance is tracked more accurately.
  3. Simplified Payouts — Fleet wallets enable bulk payout processing instead of handling individual driver withdrawals one by one.
  4. Data-Driven Decisions — Fleet-level analytics help identify trends, optimize driver allocation, and improve overall service quality.
  5. Professional Operations — Corporate fleet partners expect structured management tools. Fleet management makes your platform more attractive to business clients.
Scaling Tip

Start with fleet management once you have more than 50 active drivers. Below that threshold, direct driver management through the admin panel is usually sufficient. As you scale, introduce fleets strategically — one per major zone or business partner.
