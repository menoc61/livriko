Plans

Subscription plans allow you to configure driver commission structures and offer premium tiers that drive loyalty and revenue growth.

Why Plans Matter

Plans give you fine-grained control over how drivers interact with your platform. Instead of a single flat commission rate for every driver, you can offer multiple tiers that incentivize commitment, reward loyalty, and generate recurring subscription revenue.

Key benefits of a well-structured plan system include:

  • Commission Control — Lower commission rates for higher-tier plans encourage drivers to commit long-term.
  • Revenue Diversification — Earn subscription income on top of per-ride commissions.
  • Driver Retention — Drivers subscribed to a plan are less likely to churn because switching means losing their benefits.
  • Premium Features — Offer exclusive perks like priority ride matching, advanced analytics, or reduced cancellation penalties.

Plan Fields Reference

Every plan you create contains the following fields. Configure each one carefully to define the exact value proposition for your drivers.

Field Type Description
Name Text Display name of the plan (e.g., "Basic", "Premium", "Pro"). This is visible to drivers in the app when they browse available plans.
Description Rich Text A detailed description of what the plan includes. Explain the benefits clearly so drivers understand the value.
Price Number The subscription cost the driver pays to activate the plan. Displayed in the platform's configured currency.
Duration Select How long the plan lasts before renewal is required. Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly.
Commission Percentage Number (%) The platform commission the driver pays per ride while subscribed. This should be lower than the default (unsubscribed) commission rate.
Features List A list of premium features included in the plan (e.g., priority rides, analytics access, reduced cancellation penalties).
Status Toggle Enable or disable the plan. Disabled plans are not visible to drivers and cannot be subscribed to.

Commission vs. Subscription Model

Livriko supports a hybrid revenue model where you earn both per-ride commissions and subscription fees. Understanding how these interact is critical for pricing your plans effectively.

How It Works

When a driver subscribes to a plan, the Commission Percentage defined in the plan replaces the default platform commission for that driver. The driver continues to pay the plan subscription fee on top of the reduced per-ride commission.

Example Scenario

Consider a platform where the default commission is 25%. You create a "Premium" plan at $49/month with a 15% commission rate.

  • Without a plan, a driver earning $1,000 in rides pays $250 in commission.
  • With the Premium plan, the same driver pays $150 in commission + $49 subscription = $199 total.
  • The driver saves $51 per month, while you earn a guaranteed $49 subscription fee regardless of ride volume.
Pricing Caution

Ensure the subscription price and reduced commission still make financial sense. If the commission reduction is too steep and the subscription is too low, you may lose revenue compared to the default commission model. Model your expected ride volumes before finalizing plan prices.

Creating a Plan — Step by Step

1

Navigate to the Plans Section

Log in to your admin panel, then go to Livriko Management → Plans. You will see a list of all existing plans with their status, price, and subscriber count.

2

Click "Add New Plan"

Click the Add New Plan button in the top-right corner of the Plans page. This opens the plan creation form.

3

Fill in Plan Details

Enter the plan name, description, and price. Choose the appropriate duration (daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly) based on your target driver segment.

4

Set the Commission Rate

Define the commission percentage for this plan. This must be lower than the default platform commission to provide genuine value to subscribing drivers.

5

Add Feature Highlights

List the premium features included in this plan. These appear in the driver app to help drivers compare plans and make informed decisions.

6

Save and Enable

Toggle the plan status to Active and click Save. The plan is now visible to drivers in the app and available for subscription.

Managing Existing Plans

Once plans are created, you can perform the following actions from the Plans list page:

  • Edit — Modify plan details, pricing, or commission rates. Changes take effect for new subscriptions; existing subscribers retain their current plan until renewal.
  • Disable — Temporarily hide a plan from the driver app without deleting it. Current subscribers continue to benefit until their subscription expires.
  • Delete — Permanently remove a plan. This action cannot be undone, and any active subscribers will be moved to the default commission tier.
Deletion Warning

Deleting a plan immediately reverts all subscribers to the default commission rate. Inform affected drivers before deleting popular plans to maintain trust and reduce churn.

Best Practices

  1. Start Simple — Begin with two or three plan tiers (e.g., Free, Premium, Pro) and expand based on driver feedback and subscription data.
  2. Clear Value Proposition — Each plan should have a clear, quantifiable benefit. Drivers should immediately understand why upgrading is worth the cost.
  3. Monitor Adoption — Track subscription rates, churn, and the relationship between plan type and driver activity. Adjust pricing if adoption is lower than expected.
  4. Offer a Free Tier — Always provide a no-cost option. Drivers who cannot afford a subscription should still be able to operate on your platform at the standard commission rate.
  5. Annual Discounts — Consider offering a discounted yearly rate to lock in long-term commitments and reduce subscription churn.
  6. Promote Seasonally — Run limited-time promotions (e.g., "First month free" or "50% off annual plans") during peak seasons to boost subscriber acquisition.
Pro Tip

Use the Push Notifications feature to announce new plans or promotional discounts directly to drivers. Targeted notifications to unsubscribed drivers can significantly boost conversion rates.
